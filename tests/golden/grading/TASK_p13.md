# Task: compare the shortlisted vendors on a radar chart

`input/wms-vendor-selection.pptx` is a four-slide deck on choosing a warehouse management system. The evaluation panel's scores are in `input/wms-vendor-scores.csv`: one row per criterion, one column per vendor, each score from 1 to 5.

Add a slide after "Shortlist" (it becomes slide 4; "Recommendation" moves to slide 5) with:

- **A radar (spider) chart** of the CSV: the criteria as the chart's categories (its spokes), in the CSV's order, and one series per vendor, named as in the CSV, with the CSV's scores exactly. The chart's own data (what PowerPoint shows under Edit Data) must agree with what it draws.
- **A legend** naming the vendors, and each vendor in one of the deck's theme colours, not colours of your own.
- **A one-line takeaway title** that says what the comparison shows (a finding that names a vendor, not a label such as "Vendor scores"), on one line.
- The chart fills the slide's content area below the title, and nothing overlaps or leaves the slide.
- The other slides do not change.

Save the result as `work/wms-vendor-selection.pptx`.
